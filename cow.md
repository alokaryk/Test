<h3 data-path-to-node="13">3. PROJECT INTRODUCTION &amp; TECHNICAL PROCESS</h3>
<h4 data-path-to-node="14">3.1 Executive Summary</h4>
<p data-path-to-node="15">The project envisages the establishment of an eco-friendly herbal Dhoop Stick manufacturing unit using indigenous Desi Cow Dung (<em data-path-to-node="15" data-index-in-node="129">Gau Maya</em>) blended with organic binders (<em data-path-to-node="15" data-index-in-node="169">Jigat</em>), camphor (<em data-path-to-node="15" data-index-in-node="186">Bhimseni Kapoor</em>), Ayurvedic herbs (<em data-path-to-node="15" data-index-in-node="221">Jatamansi, Guggal, Havan Samagri</em>), and natural floral essential oils.</p>
<p data-path-to-node="16">The manufacturing plant will be situated on the applicant&rsquo;s own land of 400 Sq. Yards in Village Anandpur urf Bandpur, Tehsil Khekra, Baghpat (U.P.). The product caters to the rapidly growing market for chemical-free, charcoal-free spiritual and aromatic incense products.</p>
<h4 data-path-to-node="17">3.2 Manufacturing Process</h4>
<ol start="1" data-path-to-node="18">
<li>
<p data-path-to-node="18,0,0"><strong data-path-to-node="18,0,0" data-index-in-node="0">Raw Material Preparation:</strong> Sun-dried cow dung cakes are pulverized into a smooth, uniform fine powder using a 5 HP Heavy-Duty Pulverizer.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p data-path-to-node="18,1,0"><strong data-path-to-node="18,1,0" data-index-in-node="0">Blending &amp; Dough Preparation:</strong> Cow dung powder, Ayurvedic herbs, natural binding powder (<em data-path-to-node="18,1,0" data-index-in-node="88">Jigat</em>), and essential oil fragrances are blended thoroughly in a Sigma Powder Mixer with water to form a consistent dough.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p data-path-to-node="18,2,0"><strong data-path-to-node="18,2,0" data-index-in-node="0">Extrusion &amp; Forming:</strong> The dough is fed into an Automatic Hydraulic Dhoop Stick Extrusion Machine to produce uniform, charcoal-free dhoop sticks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p data-path-to-node="18,3,0"><strong data-path-to-node="18,3,0" data-index-in-node="0">Natural Drying:</strong> Extruded wet sticks are loaded on wooden/mesh trays and transferred to the open solar drying yard (400 Gaj plot) for 24&ndash;48 hours.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p data-path-to-node="18,4,0"><strong data-path-to-node="18,4,0" data-index-in-node="0">Quality Inspection &amp; Packaging:</strong> Dried sticks are quality-checked, batch-sealed in moisture-proof inner pouches, and packed into eco-friendly printed box packaging.</p>
</li>
</ol>
